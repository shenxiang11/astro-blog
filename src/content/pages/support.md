## 常见问题

### Mac 上按快捷键没有出现面板

默认快捷键是 ⌘⇧V。请确认没有被其他软件占用，并到妙贴设置里重新录制快捷键。

### 点击卡片后没有粘贴到上一个应用

自动粘贴需要 macOS「辅助功能」权限。未授权时，妙贴只会把内容写入剪贴板，你需要再到目标应用里手动粘贴。可在系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 辅助功能中打开妙贴。

### iPhone 上复制了新内容，历史里没有出现

iOS 不允许 App 在后台持续读取剪贴板。请打开妙贴，或点右上角「导入」，才会写入当前剪贴板。

### 复制的密码没有被记录

这是默认行为。Mac 上会跳过密码管理器标记为私密的内容，以及安全输入期间（例如密码框）的复制。可在设置里关闭「忽略密码框和私密剪贴板」。

### 如何清空历史

在设置里选择「清空未置顶历史」。置顶条目会保留。也可以在列表里单独删除某一条。

### Common questions

**The shortcut does nothing on Mac**  
The default shortcut is ⌘⇧V. Make sure another app is not using it, then record a new shortcut in MyPaste Settings.

**Clicking a card does not paste into the previous app**  
Auto-paste needs macOS Accessibility permission. Without it, MyPaste only copies to the clipboard. Enable MyPaste in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

**New copies do not appear on iPhone**  
iOS does not allow background clipboard monitoring. Open MyPaste, or tap Import, to save the current clipboard.

**Passwords are not saved**  
This is expected. On Mac, MyPaste skips items marked confidential by password managers, and copies made during secure input. You can turn this off in Settings.

**How to clear history**  
Use Clear Unpinned History in Settings. Pinned items stay. You can also delete a single item from the list.
