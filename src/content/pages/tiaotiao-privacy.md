## 我们是谁

跳跳（TiaoTiao）是一款跳绳计数 App。你可以用摄像头、AirPods 或 Apple Watch 计数，跳完后在手机上查看圆环、记录，并生成海报。

## 核心原则

我们**不收集、不上传、不出售、不与第三方共享**你的跳绳记录或个人信息。本 App **没有账号系统，没有分析统计，没有广告，没有追踪。** 核心功能不需要联网。

记录只保存在你自己的设备上。不用注册，不用登录。

## 本 App 会在设备上处理哪些内容

为了计数和展示记录，本 App 会在设备本地处理：

- 本次的下数、时长、节奏、估算消耗
- 计数方式（摄像头、AirPods 或 Apple Watch）
- 用手表或支持心率的耳机时，跳绳过程中的心率读数
- 你设定的每日目标、间歇方案，以及是否写入健康 App
- 你选择保存的跳绳记录

摄像头画面只用于当场识别身体起伏和可选的贴纸遮挡，**不会录像，也不会把画面存下来或传出去。**

## 数据存在哪里

- 跳绳记录、目标和设置保存在本机
- **不会同步到 iCloud，不会传到我们的服务器，我们这边也没有服务器存你的内容**
- 可选的「请我喝杯咖啡」走 Apple 的 App Store 内购。付款信息由 Apple 处理；本 App 只在本机记住你请过几杯

卸载 App 后，系统会删除上述本地数据。已写入健康 App 的健身记录仍由健康 App 保管，需要的话请在健康里自行删除。

## 权限说明

这些权限都按需申请，不授权也能用其余功能。

- **摄像头（可选）**：仅用于前置摄像头识别身体上下起伏，从而统计次数。画面在本机实时处理，不保存、不上传。
- **运动与健身（可选）**：用于读取 AirPods 和 Apple Watch 的运动传感器，在不看摄像头时计数。
- **健康（可选）**：
  - 读取：跳绳时显示心率（来自支持心率的耳机或已配对的手表）。
  - 写入：设置里的「写入健康 App」默认关闭。打开后，完成的跳绳可以写成健康里的跳绳健身，并附带消耗。
  - 手表：健康权限也用来保持这次运动，避免手表中途休眠。
- **照片（仅添加，可选）**：只有你主动把海报存到相册时才会用到。本 App 不能读取你的相册。

## 你能控制什么

- 不授权摄像头、运动或健康，改用其他计数方式
- 关闭「写入健康 App」
- 在记录里删除某一次跳绳
- 不生成、不保存、不分享海报
- 随时卸载本 App

## 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集儿童的个人信息。

## 政策更新

功能若发生变化（例如增加云同步、账号或统计），我们会更新本政策，并在本页面公布最新版本。

## Privacy Policy (English)

This page is the privacy policy for **TiaoTiao** (跳跳). Use this URL as the App Store Connect Privacy Policy link.

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Questions: [863461783@qq.com](mailto:863461783@qq.com)

### Who we are

TiaoTiao is a jump-rope counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. You can count with the camera, AirPods, or Apple Watch, then review rings, history, and a shareable poster.

### Our principle

We do **not** collect, upload, sell, or share your jump records or personal information. TiaoTiao has **no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.** Core features work without a network connection.

Records stay on your device. No sign-in is required.

### What the app processes on your device

To count jumps and show history, TiaoTiao locally processes:

- Jump count, duration, cadence, and estimated calories for each session
- The counting method (camera, AirPods, or Apple Watch)
- Heart-rate readings during a session when you use a Watch or heart-rate-capable headphones
- Daily goals, interval plans, and whether to write to Apple Health
- Jump records you choose to keep

The camera feed is used only in real time to detect body motion and an optional face sticker. **We do not record video, and we do not save or upload camera frames.**

### Where data is stored

- Jump records, goals, and settings are stored on-device
- **Nothing is synced to iCloud or sent to our servers. We do not operate a backend that stores your content.**
- The optional “buy me a coffee” tip uses Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment details; this app only remembers a local tip count.

Uninstalling the app removes this local data. Workouts already written to Apple Health remain in Health until you delete them there.

### Permissions

All of these are requested in context. You can decline and still use the other counting methods.

- **Camera (optional):** front camera, to detect up-and-down body motion for counting. Frames are processed on-device and are not saved or uploaded.
- **Motion & Fitness (optional):** AirPods and Apple Watch motion sensors, so you can count without the camera.
- **Health (optional):**
  - Read: heart rate during a session (from compatible headphones or a paired Watch).
  - Write: “Write to Health” is off by default. When enabled, a finished session can be saved as a Jump Rope workout with active energy.
  - Watch: Health access also keeps the workout running so the watch does not sleep mid-set.
- **Photo Library (add-only, optional):** used only when you save a poster. The app cannot read your library.

### Your controls

- Skip camera, motion, or Health and use another counting method
- Turn off writing to Health
- Delete a session from history
- Skip creating, saving, or sharing a poster
- Uninstall the app at any time

### Children

TiaoTiao is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect children’s personal information.

### Changes

If we add features that change this (for example cloud sync, accounts, or analytics), we will update this policy on this page.
