## 我们是谁

相册清理（Tidy）是一款 iPhone / iPad 相册整理 App。它从未审阅的照片和视频里抽出一小批（每轮最多 15 个），让你决定保留或删除。

## 核心原则

我们**不收集、不上传、不出售、不与第三方共享**你的照片、视频或个人信息。本 App **没有账号系统，没有分析统计，没有广告，没有追踪。** 核心审阅功能不需要把内容发到我们的服务器；我们这边也没有服务器存你的相册。

审阅记录只保存在你自己的设备上。不用注册，不用登录。

## 本 App 会在设备上处理哪些内容

为了挑选、预览和记录你的决定，本 App 会在设备本地处理：

- 系统相册里的照片和视频（用于展示缩略图、原图、实况照片和视频预览）
- 拍摄日期、体积、时长、是否收藏、是否截图等相册元数据（用于挑选本轮项目）
- 你对本轮项目的决定（保留 / 删除）
- 已审阅项目的本地标识，避免之后再出现
- 本轮进度、历史场次摘要，以及累计腾出空间等统计
- 你是否选择「包含收藏」
- 进程被中断时，用于恢复的未完成审阅 / 未确认删除记录

本 App **不会把照片或视频副本存到我们的云端**，也不会用它们做推荐或广告。

## 数据存在哪里

- 已审记录、统计、设置和未完成场次保存在本机（SwiftData / 系统偏好）
- **不会同步到我们的服务器；我们也不做独立的云同步**
- 预览 iCloud 照片或视频时，系统可能从 **Apple 的 iCloud 照片图库** 下载原文件到本机，这是系统相册能力，数据仍由 Apple 保管
- 你确认删除后，删除请求交给系统「照片」。项目会进入「最近删除」，并随 iCloud 照片同步到你的其他设备。这是 Apple 的相册同步，不是我们的上传

卸载 App 后，系统会删除上述本地审阅记录和统计。你的相册本身仍在「照片」里；已经删除并清空「最近删除」的项目，按系统规则处理。

## 权限说明

- **照片（读取与写入）**：整理相册是核心功能，需要完整相册权限才能从未审阅项目里挑选。本 App 会读取照片和视频供你审阅；只有你确认删除后，才会通过系统接口移除选中的项目。你也可以在审阅时改系统收藏状态。
- **有限访问**：若只授权部分照片，本 App 无法按「未审阅」规则正常挑选，需要到系统设置里改为完整访问。
- **网络**：仅在预览仍停在 iCloud 上的照片 / 视频时，由系统下载。本 App 不会把内容发到我们的服务器。

## 你能控制什么

- 每轮自己决定保留或删除；删除前会再确认，也可以把单张从删除列表里救回
- 默认跳过已收藏项目；需要的话可以打开「包含收藏」
- 在统计里重置已审记录，之后可以重新审阅（历史场次和已腾出空间统计会保留）
- 到系统「最近删除」恢复误删项目（约 30 天内，以系统为准）
- 随时在系统设置里关闭照片权限，或卸载本 App

## 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集儿童的个人信息。

## 政策更新

功能若发生变化（例如增加云同步、账号或统计），我们会更新本政策，并在本页面公布最新版本。

## Privacy Policy (English)

This page is the privacy policy for **Tidy** (相册清理). Use this URL as the App Store Connect Privacy Policy link.

### Who we are

Tidy is a photo and video cleanup app for iPhone and iPad. Each session picks up to 15 items you have not reviewed yet, so you can keep or delete them.

### Our principle

We do **not** collect, upload, sell, or share your photos, videos, or personal information. Tidy has **no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.** Reviewing does not send your library to our servers, and we do not operate a backend that stores your album.

Review records stay on your device. No sign-in is required.

### What the app processes on your device

To pick, preview, and remember your decisions, Tidy locally processes:

- Photos and videos in the system library (thumbnails, full images, Live Photos, and video preview)
- Album metadata such as date, file size, duration, favorite state, and whether an item is a screenshot
- Your keep / delete decisions
- Local identifiers of reviewed items, so they do not appear again
- Session progress, history summaries, and space-freed totals
- Whether to include favorites
- Incomplete review or pending-deletion state, so a session can resume after an interruption

We do **not** store copies of your photos or videos on our cloud, and we do not use them for ads or recommendations.

### Where data is stored

- Review records, stats, settings, and unfinished sessions stay on-device (SwiftData / preferences)
- **Nothing is sent to our servers, and we do not provide a separate cloud sync**
- Previewing iCloud photos or videos may download originals through **Apple’s iCloud Photos**. That is a system Photos feature; Apple holds that data
- After you confirm deletion, the system Photos app removes the items into Recently Deleted and may sync that change to your other devices via iCloud Photos. That is Apple’s library sync, not an upload by us

Uninstalling the app deletes these local review records and stats. Your library remains in Photos. Items you already deleted and purged from Recently Deleted follow system rules.

### Permissions

- **Photo Library (read and write):** cleanup is the core feature and needs full library access to pick unreviewed items. The app reads photos and videos for review. It only deletes items you confirm, through the system API. You can also change the system favorite flag while reviewing.
- **Limited Library:** if you only grant access to some photos, Tidy cannot pick a normal unreviewed session. Switch to full access in Settings.
- **Network:** used only when the system needs to download an iCloud-hosted photo or video for preview. The app does not send your content to our servers.

### Your controls

- Keep or delete each item; deletion is confirmed, and you can uncheck a thumbnail to save it
- Favorites are skipped by default; turn on “Include favorites” if you want them
- Reset reviewed records in Stats to review again (session history and space-freed totals are kept)
- Restore mistakes from Recently Deleted (about 30 days, per system rules)
- Revoke Photos access in Settings, or uninstall the app

### Children

Tidy is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect children’s personal information.

### Changes

If we add features that change this (for example cloud sync, accounts, or analytics), we will update this policy on this page.
